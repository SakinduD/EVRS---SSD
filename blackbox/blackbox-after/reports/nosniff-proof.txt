X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff on every response
=================================================

The ML after-scan's only genuine finding was ZAP's "X-Content-Type-Options
Header Missing" on /health, /health/detail and /score. It was fixed rather
than accepted.

The practical risk was close to nil: the scorer listens on loopback, only the
Node backend calls it, and no browser renders its JSON. But that reasoning
depends entirely on the current deployment, and a deployment can change
without anyone revisiting the file. The header costs one line.

Fix
---
app.py: security_headers() middleware, registered after limit_request_body so
it is the outermost of the two and therefore sets the header on every response
the service produces - not only the scored 200s, but the 401s, the 404s, the
413 from the body-size limit and the 422s from validation.

Verification
------------
Checked against the running service. Column 2 is the status code, column 3 the
response header:

  GET  /health                     200  x-content-type-options: nosniff
  GET  /health/detail no token     401  x-content-type-options: nosniff
  GET  /health/detail + token      200  x-content-type-options: nosniff
  GET  /docs                       404  x-content-type-options: nosniff
  POST /score no token             401  x-content-type-options: nosniff
  POST /score + token              200  x-content-type-options: nosniff
  POST /score invalid body         422  x-content-type-options: nosniff
  POST /score oversized length     413  x-content-type-options: nosniff

All eight carry the header. The status codes are unchanged, so the middleware
adds the header without altering what the service accepts or refuses.
